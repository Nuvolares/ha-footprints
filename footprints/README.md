# Footprints

A map of every place the people in your home have been, in the Home Assistant
side panel.

The standard map only shows where everyone is right now. Footprints keeps the
full history and draws every place each person has been. Places visited once
are faint; places they keep coming back to become more and more solid.

- Records every `person` (or chosen trackers) automatically
- Imports what the Home Assistant recorder still has on first start
- Routes between places with arrows for the direction, optionally along the streets
- Look at one day at a time, or weeks, a year, or everything
- Several map types: Home Assistant's own map, OpenStreetMap, satellite, topographic
- Choose which Home Assistant users can open it, and whose history they see
- Shade by number of visits or by time spent
- Filter by time range, hide drive-through points, change the spot size
- List of most visited places
- All data stays on your Home Assistant and is included in backups
